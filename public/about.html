Nomad bot

Trade while you travel.

A Solana trading bot for busy people. Bollinger-band mean-reversion with built-in risk limits. Non-custodial — your wallet, your keys.

Automac

Scans SOL and BTC across 1m to 1d. Alerts when %B hits the band. Opens on Drift with an auto stop-loss.

Safe by default

Per-trade risk cap. Daily loss limit. Two-perp max. Kill switch always visible. The bot can't break your rules.

Non-custodial

Your wallet signs. We never hold your funds. Fund a dedicated hot wallet with only what you'll risk.

How it works

  1. Connect a hot wallet with some SOL for gas and USDC for collateral.
  2. Pick timeframes to watch in the Alerts panel. Enable notifications.
  3. When a setup fires, tap Arm. The bot opens with your chosen leverage and a stop-loss.

What Nomad isn't

Not a guaranteed winner. Not financial advice. Automated perps trading can lose your entire deposit. Start small, on a dedicated hot wallet, with money you can afford to lose.
