What it is

Not a signals group. Not a scam. A working bot.

Nomad is a Solana-native trading bot. You run it on your own machine, connect your own wallet, pick a trading mode, and it watches the market 24/7 across multiple timeframes. When a setup fires, it opens a position on Drift Protocol (perps) or Jupiter (spot), sets a stop-loss immediately, and waits for one of two take-profit levels.

Everything is logged with its reason. Every trade shows why it fired, what the sentiment gate decided, and where the exit targets sit. You can see what the bot is doing at any moment, or step away for a week.

There is no custody. There is no middleman. Your wallet signs transactions directly against Drift and Jupiter; Nomad never holds your funds.

The engine

Four strategies, one regime detector.

Rather than one master strategy that tries to do everything, Nomad runs four specialists in parallel. A regime detector reads current volatility, trend strength, and Bollinger bandwidth every five minutes and decides which strategies are allowed to fire.

1m · 5m

Scalper BB

Mean reversion on majors (SOL, BTC, ETH). Catches quick bounces off the bands in low-volatility ranges.

15m · 1h

Swing BB

Bollinger squeeze breakouts with volume confirmation. The workhorse of Cruise mode.

1h · 4h

Trend rider

Pullback entries in established trends. Waits for the 4h trend to be clear, buys the 1h dip into the midline.

1d · spot

Macro position

Multi-day spot positions on sentiment-biased setups. Slow, patient, no perp funding drag.

Pro only

Candle bounce

Aggressive 1m/5m wick-rejection scalps. Tight stops, defined R:R. Not for beginners; gated behind the Pro toggle.

The twist

Sentiment that can veto a trade.

Every fifteen minutes, Nomad pulls crypto-sphere posts from X, runs each one through Claude to classify bullish / bearish / neutral, and aggregates into a bias score from −1 to +1. If the score disagrees with a signal, the trade is rejected and logged — not placed.

Sentiment is a gate, never a trigger. It can veto or shrink an entry; it cannot originate one. You can disable it with a toggle in Risk settings.

What Nomad isn't.

Not a guaranteed winner. Automated trading on perpetual futures can lose your entire deposit. Historical performance of any strategy does not predict future results. Backtest numbers assume liquidity and fills that may not exist in live markets.

Not custodial. We never hold your funds. If the bot or this code has a bug, the worst case is a losing trade from your wallet — never a theft of it. But fund only a dedicated hot wallet, never your main.

Not financial advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to trade any specific market at any specific time. Start on devnet or with capital you can afford to lose entirely.
